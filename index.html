About this data

  • Source: berman_stendahl_descriptors, 110 rows from Stendahl Art Galleries records, GRI 2017.M.38, Box 9, Folder 16. Image numbers refer to the page photographs.
  • The six narratives group the dataset’s descriptor classes: eye and quality (aesthetic, quality, rarity, size, form, scale, quantity, material, taste, catalogue); possession and appetite (ownership, possession, naming, appetite, affect, identity, self, return, relation); price and paperwork (price, value, valuation, customs, document, omission, status); condition and authenticity (condition, alteration, display, authenticity); law, risk and secrecy (legal, legal-risk, secrecy, clarity, political, event); character and trust (moral, trust). D086 and D087 have no class yet and are placed under possession and appetite.
  • Object groups (Pre-Columbian, Etruscan and Italic, medieval and Renaissance) were assigned from the target names and the transcriptions; objects whose culture is unclear are shown as “other or unclear”.
  • Phrases dated only by year are placed at mid-year and drawn hollow.
